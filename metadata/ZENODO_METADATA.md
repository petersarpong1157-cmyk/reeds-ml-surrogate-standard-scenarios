**Description:**  
Reproducibility materials for the study *Machine Learning Surrogate Models for Rapid Exploration of U.S. Electricity-System Futures: Evidence from NREL ReEDS Standard Scenarios*. The package supports reproduction of state-level surrogate models trained on the 2024 NREL Standard Scenarios. The study compares linear regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost for utility-scale photovoltaic capacity and net lifecycle CO2e emissions using scenario-grouped validation, 20 balanced repeated holdouts, and structured permutation importance. Raw NREL data are not redistributed; users should obtain the source data from the official OEDI record (DOI: 10.25984/2504171).
